TUESDAY, 17 NOVEMBER 2026

Six stories that moved the world — reported on Tuesday 17 November 2026
Lead — The Gulf War

U.S. STRIKES IRAN AS GULF WAR ESCALATES

American warplanes hit a missile launch site on Qeshm Island after Iran targeted U.S. personnel and assets, a U.S. military official told Reuters. Mines struck tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, and Oman rescued 24 crew from a burning gas carrier as the nine-month war at sea deepened.

President Trump said he was taking seriously a report of a possible Iranian “dirty bomb” plot in Washington. Iran denied the claim.

Europe — Ukraine

UKRAINE HITS THE CRIMEA BRIDGE

Ukrainian drones and missiles struck the bridge linking Crimea to Russia; Moscow hit back at bridges in Kyiv. Russian strikes killed 40 people in Zaporizhzhia and 26 at an aid centre.

Asia — Diplomacy

TRUMP, XI AND PUTIN TO MEET IN SHENZHEN

The first three-way summit of the U.S., Chinese and Russian leaders is set for Thursday on the sidelines of the APEC meeting in China. Trump is already in Shenzhen.

Middle East — Aid

WEST URGES ISRAEL: OPEN GAZA CROSSINGS

Britain, Australia, Canada and Italy jointly demand rapid aid access as winter rains flood shelters, the U.N. warns.

Asia — Security

JAPAN SCRAMBLES JETS AFTER RUSSIAN INCURSION

Two suspected Russian aircraft violated Japanese airspace on Tuesday; Tokyo lodged a strong protest.

Health

U.S. LOSES MEASLES-FREE STATUS

Measles is spreading without interruption, health officials say — the first loss of elimination status since 2000.
